# My Toolbox

People sometimes ask me how I study the Bible. These are the tools, books and podcasts I use most when I dig into a passage. Maybe they help you go beyond the surface too!

## Bible translations

**Herziene Statenvertaling (HSV)**

The Dutch translation I read and study with every day.

**World English Bible (WEB)**

The English translation I quote on this site. It is free to use (public domain).

## Hebrew and Greek

**[Blue Letter Bible](https://www.blueletterbible.org)**

A free website and app where you can look up every word in the original Hebrew or Greek, see what it means and find where else it is used in the Bible. This is where many of my "word study" discoveries start.

## Commentaries and books

**David Guzik, Enduring Word commentary** ([enduringword.com](https://enduringword.com))

A free, clear verse-by-verse commentary on the whole Bible.

**Eric Ortlund, *Piercing Leviathan***

A book about God's defeat of evil in the book of Job, and about the meaning of Behemoth and Leviathan.

**Sam Kastensmidt, *Christ in Creation* and *Christ from Creation***

Study guides that show how Christ can be found throughout the Old Testament.

**Mart-Jan Paul**

A Dutch Old Testament scholar whose publications help me with background and context.

**Scott Noegel**

A scholar of the Bible and the ancient Near East, whose work helps me understand the world the Bible was written in.

## Podcasts

**[The Naked Bible Podcast](https://nakedbiblepodcast.com)** with Dr. Michael S. Heiser

Deep, scholarly but very accessible episodes on the Old Testament and its ancient context, including the background of the book of Job.
